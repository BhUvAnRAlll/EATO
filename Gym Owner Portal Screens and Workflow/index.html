EATO Gym Owner portal: screens and workflow

Captured from the running prototype at phone width, signed in as the Gym owner demo account (FitZone Fitness). Click any image to open it full size. The full written workflow is in OWNER_PORTAL_WORKFLOW.md.

1. Switching in and Home

Switch account (demo) → Gym owner → Home: stat tiles, in the gym now, quick actions (Invite · Announce · Event · Notify), a Needs-your-attention list that opens the fixing screen, new members per month, coaching team, next event.

2. Members

Approve or decline join requests, change roles, remove members (owner protected). Renewals lists every package by days left with renew and reminder actions.

3. Check-in (QR attendance)

Scan member QR (simulated) → Verification → Check in → Success → Scan next. Failed scans (inactive, already in, not a member, bad code) are recorded. Today's list and visit details, plus the gym's check-out QR and a front-desk display.

4. Community

A map of everything members see: gym feed, announcements, events, gym assistant, member notifications, invites, rules and permissions. Each card has a main action and a manage button.

5. Review (moderation)

Toggle review-before-publish. Approve or reject pending member posts. Keep or remove reported posts; EATO's super admins see the same reports.

6. Gym

Links to every gym-level screen: profile, subscription (no payment), trainers, members, invitations, announcements, settings, assistant, invite, check-out QR, and the public gym page as the owner sees it.
